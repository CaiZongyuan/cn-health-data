# laboratory-cn

`catalog.csv` is a project-authored curated catalog for the laboratory and
vital-sign concepts exercised by Synthea and ClinMesh. CN Health Data
contributors wrote the Chinese displays, categories, result types, preferred
UCUM units, and curation notes in this file.

The `http://loinc.org` codes identify external standard concepts. This catalog
does not claim to be the official complete LOINC Chinese linguistic variant and
does not copy that package. The repository's MIT License applies to the original
catalog selection and descriptions; it does not alter any terms applicable to
the LOINC identifiers or other third-party standards.
